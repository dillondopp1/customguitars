Our Promise

Every Obsidian is cut, carved, and voiced by hand.

We start from CNC-roughed blanks for dimensional accuracy, then every neck carve, fret dress, and nitrocellulose finish is finished entirely by hand in our shop. No two instruments leave our bench alike — and that's the point.

From wood selection to final setup, one small team of luthiers sees every build through, start to finish.
