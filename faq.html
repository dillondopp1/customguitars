Ordering & pricing

Base prices are $4,800 for the Tephra, $5,200 for the Rhyolite, $5,900 for the Basalt and $6,400 for the Scoria. Upgrades for wood grade, finish, hardware, inlays and configuration are listed in the Custom Shop. Most finished builds land between $5,500 and $8,000.

A 30% deposit reserves your build slot once you approve your written spec and fixed quote. It is fully refundable until your wood is cut, less the cost of any parts already ordered for your build; once construction begins it is non-refundable. The remaining 70% is due when your guitar passes final inspection, before it ships.

We build 12 guitars a quarter. A slot is reserved when your deposit is paid. Slots for each quarter open to the waitlist first; join it at the bottom of any page.

Every Obsidian is numbered in the order it’s completed, starting at No. 1. Numbers 1 to 25, in any model and any spec, form the Founders’ Run. They get a brass number plate and free setups for life, and the run closes for good after No. 25.

No. Every Obsidian is built to order for one player, so there’s no stock. Join the waitlist to hear when the next build slots open.

The build

Typically 12 to 16 weeks from deposit to shipping, depending on finish (nitro needs longer to cure) and inlay work. Your quote states a target ship month.

Quench is how we make every neck: maple roasted in our own kiln, laminated from five quartersawn strips with alternating grain, then rested at least 60 days before it is hand-carved. It’s our own workshop process, not a patent or a certification. More on Quench.
