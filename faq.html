Ordering & pricing

Base prices are $4,600 for the Andesite, $4,800 for the Tephra, $5,200 for the Rhyolite, $5,900 for the Basalt and $6,400 for the Scoria. The first 25 instruments, the Founders’ Run, have lower base prices (see below). Upgrades for wood grade, finish, hardware, inlays and configuration are listed in the Custom Shop. Most finished builds land between $5,500 and $8,000.

A 30% deposit reserves your build slot once you approve your written spec and fixed quote. It is fully refundable until your components are ordered, less the cost of any parts already ordered for your build; once construction begins it is non-refundable. The remaining 70% is due when your guitar passes final inspection, before it ships.

We build 6 guitars a quarter. A slot is reserved when your deposit is paid. Slots for each quarter open to the waitlist first; join it at the bottom of any page.

Every Obsidian is numbered in the order it’s completed, starting at No. 1. Numbers 1 to 25, in any model and any spec, form the Founders’ Run. Founders’ Run base prices are $3,900 for the Andesite, $4,100 for the Tephra, $4,400 for the Rhyolite, $5,000 for the Basalt and $5,400 for the Scoria; upgrades are charged at list. It’s a launch price for the first 25 players, who are taking a chance on a new builder. They also get a low number, a brass number plate and free setups for life. At 6 build slots a quarter, the run will take about a year to fill, and it closes for good after No. 25.

The build

Typically 16 to 24 weeks from deposit to shipping, plus about 4 weeks for a nitrocellulose finish, which needs longer to cure. Inlay work can add time too. Your quote states a target ship month.

Quench is our standard for every neck, maple or mahogany. Each neck is made to our profile specs or to your measurements. Then, in our shop, the frets are leveled, crowned and polished, the nut is cut and fitted by hand, and the neck is finished and set up to your measurements. It’s a standard we hold ourselves to, not a patent or a certification. More on Quench.
