FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How long does a custom build take? +

Most custom builds take 6 to 9 weeks from deposit to shipment, depending on specification complexity and our current queue. Highly detailed finishes or inlay work may extend this.

How much does a custom Obsidian guitar cost? +

Custom builds start at $4,200 for the Apex and typically range from $4,500 to $8,500 depending on model, tonewoods, hardware, and finish complexity.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes. A 50% deposit reserves your build slot in our queue. The remaining balance is collected securely before your instrument ships.

Can I order a stock (non-custom) model?

Yes — we keep a small number of completed builds in stock. Check the Models page or contact us for current availability.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship worldwide via insured freight carriers. International orders may be subject to import duties and taxes collected on delivery.

What warranty comes with an Obsidian guitar?

Every instrument includes a lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects for the original owner. See our Warranty Policy for full details.

Can I change my spec after I've placed a deposit?

Minor changes are usually possible before your build enters the shop. Once construction has started, changes may affect price and timeline — your luthier will always confirm with you first.

Do you offer financing?

We're adding financing options at checkout soon. In the meantime, our 50/50 deposit structure lets you spread payment across your build timeline.
