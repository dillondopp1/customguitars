The Obsidian artist program

We don’t pay for endorsements and we don’t hand out free guitars for social posts. We work with players who will put an instrument through real use and tell us the truth about it.

What the program is

Who itWho it’s for: working players who tour, record or teach regularly. Following size doesn’t matter. How much you play does.rsquo;s for: working players who tour, record or teach regularly, whatever the size of their following.

  • An artist rate

    Accepted artists build at a reduced artist rate, agreed case by case, with a priority build slot in the next open quarter.

  • Candid written feedback

    In return we ask for a written report after a tour, a session or six months of use. Good or bad. We’ve changed specs because of these.

  • Your voice, unedited

    If you choose to make content with your guitar, it’s yours. We’ll ask before sharing it, and we never script what you say.
