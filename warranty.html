Warranty

Lifetime Warranty

Every Obsidian guitar is covered by a lifetime warranty against defects in materials and workmanship, for as long as the original purchaser owns the instrument.

What's Covered

Structural defects in the neck, body, or finish arising from manufacturing; electronics failures due to faulty wiring or components; fret and hardware defects present at delivery.

What's Not Covered

Normal wear (fret wear, string corrosion, finish checking from humidity/temperature changes), damage from accidents, improper storage, modification by a third party, or neglect.

How To Make A Claim

Email warranty@obsidianguitars.com with your serial number, proof of purchase, and photos of the issue. We'll respond within three business days with next steps, which may include local repair reimbursement or return-to-shop service.
