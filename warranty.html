Warranty

Lifetime warranty

Every Obsidian guitar is covered by a lifetime warranty against defects in materials and workmanship for as long as the original owner owns it.

What’s covered

Structural defects in the neck or body that come from how we built it, including a Quench neck that moves beyond normal truss-rod adjustment. Finish defects not caused by environment or impact. Electronics failures caused by faulty wiring or components. Fret or hardware defects present at delivery.

What’s not covered

Normal wear such as fret wear, hardware plating wear and string corrosion; finish checking or cracking from rapid temperature or humidity changes; accidents, impact or improper storage; modifications or repairs by a third party.

Second owners

If you buy an Obsidian secondhand, register it with us using the serial number in its Provenance File. We’ll honour a five-year warranty from the original ship date and keep its build record linked to you.

Making a claim

Use our contact form (subject: “Repair or warranty”) with your serial number and photos of the issue. We’ll reply within three business days. Depending on the issue, that may mean reimbursing a repair by a qualified local luthier or a return to our shop at our cost.
