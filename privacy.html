Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Obsidian Instruments Co. (“Obsidian Guitars”, “we”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

What you type into our forms: name, email, optional phone and country, your build preferences, and messages. If you join the waitlist, your email address.

How forms are processed

Form submissions are delivered to us through a form-processing service (Netlify Forms or Formspree, depending on our hosting), which stores them on our behalf. They are not used for advertising.

How we use it

To reply to you, prepare your spec and quote, build and ship your guitar, keep its build record, and send waitlist emails if you asked for them. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Payments

Deposits and balances are processed by a third-party payment provider. We never see or store your full card details.

Cookies & analytics

This site does not set advertising cookies. If we add privacy-friendly analytics, we’ll list it here.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion at any time via our contact form. Waitlist emails include an unsubscribe link.
