Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Obsidian Instruments Co. ("Obsidian Guitars," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

Contact details you submit through our forms (name, email, phone), build preferences you share with our Custom Shop, and basic analytics data about how visitors use this site.

How We Use It

To respond to inquiries, process custom build orders and payments (handled securely by our payment processor), and improve our site and products. We do not sell your personal information.

Payment Information

Checkout and deposit payments are processed by a third-party payment processor. We never store your full card details on our servers.
