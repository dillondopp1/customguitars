      What happens after you send a request

      Six steps, and you can stop at any point before the deposit without paying anything.

      1. Build request

        The form above. Five minutes, no commitment.

      2. Spec call

        Thirty to sixty minutes with the luthier who will build your guitar. We talk about how you play, not what to buy.

      3. Written spec & fixed quote

        Every dimension, wood, part and finish in writing, with photos of candidate boards. The price on the quote is the price you pay.

      4. Deposit reserves your slot

        A 30% deposit reserves your build slot once you approve your written spec and fixed quote. Your neck blank is pulled from a Quench batch that has already rested.

      5. The build, with a journal

        One luthier, start to finish. You get a private build journal with photos at each stage.

      6. Final inspection, balance, shipping

        A 37-point setup and inspection, then the balance, then fully insured shipping with your Provenance File in the case.

      Base prices, and what each upgrade costs

      Every base price includes a Quench neck, stainless frets, locking tuners, hand-cut bone or graphite nut, the full 37-point setup, a hardshell case and the Provenance File. Most finished builds land between $5,500 and $8,000.
