How It Works

From request to road-ready.

  1. Consultation

    We review your request and schedule a call to talk tone, feel, and budget.

  2. Spec & Quote

    You receive a full written spec sheet and fixed price quote, plus wood and finish photos.

  3. Deposit & Build Slot

    A 50% deposit reserves your build slot in our queue, typically 2–4 weeks out.

  4. Hand-Built, Start To Finish

    Your guitar is cut, carved, finished, wired, and set up by one luthier from start to finish.

  5. Final Setup & Ship

    Final balance is collected, your instrument is fully set up, inspected, and shipped insured.
