Terms of sale

Last updated: October 2026

These terms apply to custom instruments ordered from Obsidian Instruments Co. (“Obsidian Guitars”, “we”).

Built to order

Every Obsidian instrument is built to order for one customer. We do not build or hold stock instruments.

Instrument numbering

Every instrument is numbered in the order it is completed, in a single sequence across all models, beginning at No. 1. Numbers are never reused or reassigned. Your instrument’s number is stamped in the neck heel and recorded in our build register and its Provenance File.

Materials

We do not use ivory or any material from protected or endangered species in our instruments. Reclaimed materials, such as wood salvaged from antique pianos, are recorded in the instrument’s build record.

Quotes

Prices on this site are base prices. Before any payment you receive a written spec and a fixed quote. The quoted price is the price you pay, unless you request a change that we confirm with you in writing.

Deposits & payment

A 30% deposit reserves your build slot once you approve your written spec and fixed quote. Until construction begins it is refundable, less the cost of any components already ordered for your build; once construction begins it is non-refundable. The remaining 70% is due when your guitar passes final inspection, before it ships.

Payments are processed by our third-party payment provider. We never see or store your full card details.

Changes to your spec

You can change your spec until the relevant stage of the build begins. Changes that affect price or timeline are confirmed in writing before we proceed.

Build times

Your quote states a target ship month. Typical builds take 16–24 weeks from deposit, plus about 4 weeks for a nitrocellulose finish. If we expect to miss your target by more than four weeks, we’ll tell you as soon as we know, and you may cancel for a refund of your deposit less the cost of parts already ordered for your build.

Shipping & risk of loss

All instruments ship fully insured. Risk of loss passes to you on delivery confirmation by the carrier. Import duties and taxes are your responsibility and are estimated in your quote where possible.

Inspection & returns

Custom instruments are built to your specification and are not returnable for change of mind. If your guitar arrives damaged or doesn’t match your approved spec, contact us within 7 days of delivery and we’ll make it right under our warranty.

Limitation of liability

To the extent permitted by law, our liability is limited to the purchase price of the instrument.

Governing law
