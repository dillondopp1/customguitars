Our Process

Built like it has to last fifty years. Because it does.

Obsidian Guitars was founded on a simple belief: a guitar you'll play for decades deserves to be built by hands, not just machines. Our small team of luthiers oversees every instrument from rough lumber to final setup.

Step By Step

The build, start to finish.

  1. Wood Selection

    Every billet is hand-selected and weighed, tapped, and inspected for grain consistency before it's approved for a build.

  2. CNC Roughing

    Precision CNC machining establishes dimensional accuracy for the body contour, neck pocket, and control cavities — the only step where a machine touches your guitar.

  3. Hand Carving & Shaping

    Top carves, neck profiles, and heel contours are shaped entirely by hand, checked constantly with calipers and radius gauges.

  4. Fretwork

    Frets are pressed, leveled, crowned, and polished by hand for buzz-free action, string to string, fret to fret.

  5. Finishing

    Nitrocellulose or polyurethane finishes are hand-sprayed in thin coats, wet-sanded, and hand-buffed to a mirror gloss or satin sheen.

  6. Hand Wiring

    Every pickup is wound and wired in-house, point-to-point, and tested on the bench before installation.

  7. 37-Point Setup

    Action, intonation, pickup height, nut slots, and electronics are checked against 37 individual quality points before your guitar is approved to ship.

Materials

Tonewoods selected for how they sound, not just how they look.

We source alder, swamp ash, mahogany, and figured maple from sustainably managed mills, and every billet is dried, weighed, and tap-tested for resonance before it earns a place on an Obsidian guitar.

We maintain relationships with small sawmills in the Pacific Northwest and Appalachia, prioritizing responsibly harvested lumber over volume.
