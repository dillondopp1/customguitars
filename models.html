What stays the same from model to model

The prices differ because some bodies take more work than others. These three things are identical on all five.

  • The neck

    Every model gets a Quench neck, made to your profile spec, with the frets, nut and setup done by hand in our shop.

  • The bench

    One person finishes, frets and sets up your guitar, and signs the build sheet.

  • The paperwork

    Serial number, wood record and a signed build sheet ship with every instrument.

If none of the five is quite right, start from the closest one and tell us what to change

Neck shapes, pickups, string counts and finishes can all change on the spec call.
